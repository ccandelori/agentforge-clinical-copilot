# ACC/AHA 2017 Hypertension Guidelines — BP Categories and Targets (Summary)

> **SOURCE:** Whelton PK, Carey RM, Aronow WS, et al. *2017
> ACC/AHA/AAPA/ABC/ACPM/AGS/APhA/ASH/ASPC/NMA/PCNA Guideline for the
> Prevention, Detection, Evaluation, and Management of High Blood
> Pressure in Adults*. Hypertension. 2018;71(6):e13–e115.
>
> **NOTICE:** Short factual summary for the AgentForge demo corpus.
> Not clinical advice. Refer to the source for the full guidance.

## Blood Pressure Categories (in mmHg)

The 2017 ACC/AHA guideline classifies adult BP into four categories
based on the higher of systolic (SBP) or diastolic (DBP):

| Category               | SBP        |     | DBP       |
|------------------------|------------|-----|-----------|
| Normal                 | <120       | and | <80       |
| Elevated               | 120–129    | and | <80       |
| Hypertension Stage 1   | 130–139    | or  | 80–89     |
| Hypertension Stage 2   | ≥140       | or  | ≥90       |

A diagnosis of hypertension is made when the average of two or more
properly measured readings on two or more occasions falls into stage
1 or stage 2 categories.

## Treatment Targets

For most adults with hypertension and confirmed BP ≥130/80 mmHg, the
target is **<130/80 mmHg**.

Stratification by 10-year ASCVD risk:

- **Stage 1 hypertension (130–139/80–89) without ASCVD or 10-year
  ASCVD risk ≥10%:** lifestyle therapy first; reassess in 3–6 months.
- **Stage 1 hypertension with clinical ASCVD or 10-year ASCVD risk
  ≥10%, or with diabetes / CKD:** lifestyle therapy *plus*
  pharmacologic therapy.
- **Stage 2 hypertension (≥140/≥90):** lifestyle therapy plus
  pharmacologic therapy in all adults.

## First-Line Pharmacologic Options

Initial first-line therapy for non-Black adults includes:

- Thiazide diuretics
- Calcium channel blockers
- ACE inhibitors or ARBs (do NOT combine ACEi with ARB)

For Black adults without HF or CKD, initial therapy includes
thiazides or CCBs.

## Special Populations

- **Patients with CKD (stage 3 or higher, or stage 1–2 with
  albuminuria):** ACE inhibitors or ARBs are preferred.
- **Patients with diabetes:** any of the four classes is acceptable;
  ACEi/ARB if albuminuria is present.
- **Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction:** GDMT (e.g.,
  ACEi/ARB/ARNI plus beta blocker plus aldosterone antagonist).

## Out-of-Office Confirmation

Before initiating pharmacotherapy for newly diagnosed stage 1
hypertension, confirmation by out-of-office BP measurements (home BP
monitoring or 24-hour ambulatory BP monitoring) is recommended to
avoid white-coat misclassification.
