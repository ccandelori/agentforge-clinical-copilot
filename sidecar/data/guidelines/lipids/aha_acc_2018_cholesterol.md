>

## Four Major Statin Benefit Groups

The 2018 guideline identifies four groups for whom statin therapy is
recommended:

1. **Adults with clinical ASCVD** (acute coronary syndromes, history
   of MI, stable or unstable angina, coronary or other arterial
   revascularization, stroke, TIA, or peripheral arterial disease).
2. **Adults aged ≥21 with primary severe LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL**.
3. **Adults aged 40–75 with diabetes and LDL-C 70–189 mg/dL**.
4. **Adults aged 40–75 without diabetes, with LDL-C 70–189 mg/dL,
   and 10-year ASCVD risk ≥7.5%** based on the Pooled Cohort
   Equations.

## Intensity of Statin Therapy

| Intensity     | Expected LDL-C reduction | Examples                           |
|---------------|--------------------------|------------------------------------|
| High          | ≥50%                     | Atorvastatin 40–80 mg, Rosuvastatin 20–40 mg |
| Moderate      | 30–49%                   | Atorvastatin 10–20 mg, Rosuvastatin 5–10 mg, Simvastatin 20–40 mg |
| Low           | <30%                     | Simvastatin 10 mg, Pravastatin 10–20 mg |

## Treatment Recommendations

- **Clinical ASCVD, age ≤75:** high-intensity statin (or maximum
  tolerated intensity).
- **Clinical ASCVD, age >75:** moderate- or high-intensity statin
  after risk discussion.
- **Primary severe LDL-C ≥190:** high-intensity statin without
  10-year risk assessment.
- **Diabetes, age 40–75:** moderate-intensity statin; add high-
  intensity if multiple ASCVD risk enhancers.
- **Primary prevention, age 40–75, ASCVD risk 7.5–<20%:** moderate-
  intensity statin after risk discussion.
- **Primary prevention, age 40–75, ASCVD risk ≥20%:** high-intensity
  statin to reduce LDL-C ≥50%.

## Risk Enhancers

Risk enhancers that may favor statin therapy in borderline cases
include:

- Family history of premature ASCVD
- Persistently elevated LDL-C ≥160 mg/dL
- Chronic kidney disease
- Metabolic syndrome
- Conditions specific to women (preeclampsia, premature menopause)
- Inflammatory diseases (psoriasis, rheumatoid arthritis, HIV)
- Ethnicity (e.g., South Asian)
- Persistently elevated triglycerides ≥175 mg/dL
- ABI <0.9, hs-CRP ≥2.0 mg/L, Lp(a) ≥50 mg/dL, apoB ≥130 mg/dL

## Coronary Artery Calcium

In adults age 40–75 at borderline (5 to <7.5%) or intermediate
(≥7.5 to <20%) 10-year ASCVD risk where the decision is uncertain,
measuring CAC may help refine risk:

- **CAC = 0:** withholding statin therapy is reasonable (unless
  diabetes, family history of premature CHD, or smoking).
- **CAC 1–99:** statin therapy is favored, especially for those age
  ≥55.
- **CAC ≥100 or ≥75th percentile:** statin therapy is indicated.
