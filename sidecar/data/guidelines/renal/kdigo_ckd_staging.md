# KDIGO CKD Staging — GFR and Albuminuria Categories (Summary)

> **SOURCE:** *KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline for the
> Evaluation and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease*. Kidney
> International Supplements. https://kdigo.org/guidelines/ckd/
>
> **NOTICE:** Short factual summary for the AgentForge demo corpus.
> Not clinical advice. Refer to the source for the full guidance.

## Definition

CKD is defined as abnormalities of kidney structure or function,
present for **>3 months**, with implications for health.

Diagnostic criteria require ≥1 of:

- GFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m² for >3 months
- Markers of kidney damage (one or more):
  - Albuminuria (AER ≥30 mg/24h or ACR ≥30 mg/g)
  - Urine sediment abnormalities
  - Electrolyte and other abnormalities due to tubular disorders
  - Histologic abnormalities
  - Structural abnormalities on imaging
  - History of kidney transplantation

## GFR Categories

| Category | GFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) | Description                       |
|----------|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| G1       | ≥90                  | Normal or high                    |
| G2       | 60–89                | Mildly decreased                  |
| G3a      | 45–59                | Mildly to moderately decreased    |
| G3b      | 30–44                | Moderately to severely decreased  |
| G4       | 15–29                | Severely decreased                |
| G5       | <15                  | Kidney failure                    |

GFR categories alone do not define CKD without markers of damage —
G1 and G2 require an additional marker (e.g., albuminuria).

## Albuminuria Categories

| Category | ACR (mg/g) | ACR (mg/mmol) | Description                  |
|----------|------------|---------------|------------------------------|
| A1       | <30        | <3            | Normal to mildly increased   |
| A2       | 30–300     | 3–30          | Moderately increased         |
| A3       | >300       | >30           | Severely increased           |

The preferred specimen is a **first-morning void**, with quantitative
albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR) reported. Random spot collections
are acceptable when first-morning void isn't available.

## Combined Risk Stratification

Risk of progression and adverse outcomes is stratified by the
combination of GFR and albuminuria:

- **Low risk:** G1–G2 with A1 (and no other markers)
- **Moderately increased risk:** G1–G2 with A2; G3a with A1
- **High risk:** G1–G2 with A3; G3a with A2; G3b with A1
- **Very high risk:** G3a with A3; G3b with A2 or A3; G4–G5 with any

Higher-risk categories are referred to nephrology and warrant more
frequent monitoring.

## Practical Notes

- The GFR equation recommended is the **2021 CKD-EPI creatinine
  equation** (not race-corrected). The cystatin C equation is
  preferred when greater accuracy is needed.
- A single low GFR is not CKD — confirm chronicity (>3 months) before
  staging.
- Albuminuria progression and stable GFR can both indicate
  progressive CKD; track BOTH markers over time.
